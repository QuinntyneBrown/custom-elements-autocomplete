# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Custom Elements Autocomplete welcomes participants of every background and experience level. Everyone should be able to contribute in an environment that respects their dignity and allows them to learn, ask questions, and disagree constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Treat participants with respect and give feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and remain open to corrections.
- Help others participate, including people new to the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and their effects on others.
- Respect privacy and use others' work with appropriate permission and attribution.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project community spaces. It also applies when a participant represents the project publicly.

## Report a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to [quinntynebrown@gmail.com](mailto:quinntynebrown@gmail.com). Describe what happened, when and where it occurred, and any relevant evidence. Share only information needed to assess the concern.

Reports will be handled with discretion; information may be shared as needed to investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Do not post incident details or personal information in public issues.

Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for applying this policy. They may remove inappropriate content, ask a participant to change their behavior, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. The response should reflect the severity, impact, and recurrence of the behavior.

Where practical, maintainers will explain a moderation decision privately to the affected participant. A participant may request reconsideration through the same private contact and provide relevant context. A maintainer directly involved in a reported incident should defer assessment to another uninvolved maintainer when one is available.
